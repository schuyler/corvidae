Goals are your work queue, and the beat shows the front of it: up to five open or in-progress goals, front of the queue first. Work the front: if a goal is marked in-progress, resume it; otherwise take the first listed goal and advance it. If a goal is done, retire it. Don't take up again a goal you retired unless something new has changed. When the listed goals are all dead ends, call list_goals - blocked and waiting goals stay on the board even though the beat hides them. Act only when the assessment warrants it — a beat with nothing to advance is fine.
